Phase 4: data display and feedback components.

- Data display: Card (outline, elevated, filled; link or button card with a stretched target; selected and disabled), Badge, Tag (static, toggle with aria-pressed, removable), Avatar (initials, icon, image, online dot), List and ListRow.
- Feedback: Alert (inline and banner; opt-in announcement by tone), ToastProvider and useToast (at most three, 5 s default, Danger and toasts with an action stay), ProgressBar, Spinner, Skeleton and SkeletonListRow, EmptyState.
- Motion: toast slide-in and swipe, spinner rotation (pulses with reduced motion), skeleton shimmer. Button and IconButton spinners use the same animation.
- Storybook: stories with the Figma matrices and a "Patient list" pattern.
